Built for the era of AI copilots

Integrity is knowing who is at the keyboard — not policing what they use.

A new generation of cheating tools advertises "runs invisibly during your interview." We treat that as a category we beat — without making candidates feel watched.

What Aptura blocks

  • Browser overlay copilots that whisper answers during screen-sharing.
  • Virtual cameras & deepfakes spoofing identity at the lens.
  • Secondary monitors or screen-sharing apps quietly relaying questions.
  • Synthetic voice generated audio or lips-out-of-sync with speech.
  • Multiple faces, phones in frame, off-screen prompters.
  • Identity proxies — a different person sitting the live interview.

What Aptura does not do

  • No real-time human watcher. Reviewers only see flagged events, after the fact.
  • No raw video or audio leaves the browser. Detectors run on-device; only typed events are sent.
  • No emotion or affect inference. No "candidate looked stressed" scoring. Ever.
  • No identity matching beyond the ID check. No voiceprints, no face match against other databases.
  • No keystroke surveillance for content. We track tab-switches, not what you type elsewhere.
  • Encrypted at rest. Deleted on request. Right-to-erase honored across every Aptura artifact.

Evidence-based scoring

Every score points to a quoted line and a timestamp.

No "AI vibes." Aptura's Core 6 rubric reads the transcript, picks the candidate's own words as the evidence, and shows them next to the score — so a reviewer can disagree with the rating and see exactly what informed it.

  • The Aptura Core 6 — Problem framing · Communication · Domain knowledge · Tradeoff reasoning · Decision quality · Integrity. Published and versioned.
  • Quoted evidence per score. Disagree with the rating? The proof is right there.
  • Re-score on demand. New role, new lens — same evidence, recalculated.
  • Bias-aware by design. Third-party audit (NYC AEDT-144 methodology or equivalent) is scheduled before public launch.

Advisory by design

AI recommends. Humans decide.

An interview report is a recommendation, not a verdict. The hiring manager signs every advance or decline — and the audit trail keeps both sides accountable.

Aptura recommends

The system produces a transparent, evidence-backed recommendation. Strong evidence is labelled "Advance." Weak or thin evidence is labelled "Hold" — never "Reject."

  • Score, recommendation, and reasoning visible side-by-side
  • Aptura Core 6 rubric drives every rating
  • Transcript quotes anchor every claim
  • Integrity events are surfaced, not buried

Your hiring manager decides

The recruiter or hiring manager reviews the evidence and signs the decision. Aptura never auto-rejects — and every decision is logged with the reviewer's name and reason.

  • Override the recommendation in one click
  • Reason is captured for the candidate's outcome message

Questions, answered

The objections recruiters and candidates ask first.

CompanyWhat's actually different about Aptura's interview vs. existing AI video tools?
It's a live, fullscreen-locked, identity-verified interview with on-device proctoring — not an async video upload. Every score points to a quoted transcript line. Reports include an integrity timeline with severity per event.
CandidateWill a real person watch me during the interview?
No. There is no real-time human watcher. Detectors run on your device; only typed events are sent. Reviewers only see flagged events, after the fact, with the recording encrypted at rest.
CompanyHow is bias handled?
Aptura's scoring model is bias-aware by design: rubric-driven scoring, evidence-linked ratings, advisory recommendations only, and a human signs every outcome. A third-party audit (NYC AEDT-144 methodology or equivalent) is scheduled before public launch.
CandidateWhat if I have a disability or need an accommodation?
Accommodations are first-class. Extended time, captions, screen-reader-friendly question delivery, and alternative response modes are all available at request — they do not affect your score or appear in your report.
CompanyWhat does "human decides" mean operationally?
Aptura recommends Advance or Hold — never auto-rejects. A named reviewer signs every outcome. Each decision is logged with the reviewer's name, the evidence shown, and the reason given; retention is configurable per pilot.
CandidateCan I retake the interview if my connection drops?
Yes. Connection drops are not penalised. If a session is interrupted unexpectedly, you'll get a one-tap re-entry and a fresh recording — without losing prior responses.
CompanyWhat happens to the recording, transcript, and proctoring events?
Recordings are encrypted at rest. Retention is configurable per pilot. Right-to-erase is honored across every Aptura artifact — recording, transcript, scoring, and decision metadata.
CandidateWill I get feedback even if I'm not advanced?
Yes. Every applicant — advanced or not — receives an outcome message with a competency-level note, the recommendation reason, and an option to request a re-score for a different role.
CompanyCan I bring our own rubric?
Yes — custom rubrics are part of the pilot onboarding. The Aptura Core 6 is the default; you can adapt it to your role and we'll apply the same evidence-linked scoring approach.
CandidateDoes Aptura analyse my face for emotion?
No. We do not infer emotion, affect, or personality from your face or voice. We detect presence, identity match, and proctoring signals — never feelings.
CompanyHow does it integrate with our ATS?
ATS integrations (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SuccessFactors) are on the roadmap. The core product runs standalone today; pilots typically start with email and CSV handoff into your existing flow. Tell us the ATS you use and we'll build that integration next.
CandidateCan I practice before the real interview?
Yes. A full practice round mirrors the real one — same UI, same rubric, no scoring against you. You'll see exactly what's being evaluated before you sit the real interview.
CompanyWhat if a HIGH-severity event fires by mistake?
A HIGH-severity auto-end can be appealed. The reviewer sees the clip and the reason; if the event was a false positive, the candidate is offered a fresh interview at no cost to either side.
CandidateI don't have a webcam. Can I still apply?
A working camera and microphone are required for the proctored interview by design. We'll guide you through low-bandwidth and mobile options. Accommodations are honored.
CompanyCan we white-label the candidate experience?
White-labelling the candidate flow (your branding, your domain) is planned for the post-launch enterprise tier. In a pilot, candidates always see the role and the company name clearly.
CandidateHow long is the interview?
Most Aptura interviews run between 18 and 35 minutes — sized to the rubric for the role. You'll see the expected duration before you start, and there are no surprise rounds.
