# Aptura — Positioning & Verbal Identity

> Working name **Aptura** (clear trademark at launch — an "Aptura AI" software co + "Aptura Group"
> exist). Brand for the unified AI hiring platform. Pairs with `aptura-visual-identity.md`.
> **Repositioned 2026-06-20** (see `../superpowers/v2/2026-06-20-proctored-integrity.md`): the differentiator
> shifted from "no surveillance" to **rigorous proctoring → results you can trust.** No-ghosting + merit kept.

## One-liner
Aptura is a unified hiring platform where candidates find jobs, take a **rigorously proctored** AI interview,
and **always hear back** — and companies get screening results they can **actually trust.**

## Tagline
**Primary — "Get seen. Get interviewed. Get hired."** (the candidate's journey, in three beats.)

Supporting lines (campaign / context):
- "Apply, interview, hear back — every time." — the no-ghosting promise.
- "A pass that proves it." / "Real skills, really verified." — the proctored-trust promise.
- "Find your fit. Earn your shot." — job-fit + a fair, level field.

## Positioning statement
For job seekers who want a fair shot and hiring teams drowning in unverifiable applications and AI-assisted
cheating, **Aptura** is a unified hiring platform that runs every candidate through a **rigorously proctored**
AI interview, **guarantees a real outcome and feedback**, and evaluates on **merit** — so a result is one you
can **trust.** Unlike job boards that ghost applicants and screening tools whose results can be gamed, Aptura
is two-sided, proctored, and built so a pass means something.

## Messaging pillars
1. **Answered, always** — every applicant gets a real outcome + feedback (the no-ghosting guarantee).
2. **Cheat-proof** — a strictly proctored interview (camera + mic required, no mute, full integrity monitoring);
   serious cheating is caught and flagged in real time. A pass is *earned*, the same way for everyone.
3. **On merit** — fair, explainable, evidence-based evaluation; a human always decides.
4. **Two-sided & intelligent** — a real marketplace *and* an adaptive, proctored AI interview, in one place.

## Voice & tone
Confident, clear, human, honest. Plain over jargon; optimistic without hype. Candidates are people —
never "resources" or "headcount." **Upfront about proctoring:** the interview is monitored and recorded, and
we say so plainly — rigor you can see, applied equally to everyone, never hidden. No dark patterns. Short
sentences. We say what we do and mean it: the brand's credibility *is* the product's promise.

## Audience framing
- **Candidates:** "Take a fair, proctored interview where everyone plays by the same rules — and you'll always hear back."
- **Companies:** "Screen the flood on merit, with results you can trust — and never ghost a candidate again."

## Naming note
Aptura is the **working** name. Before any public launch: run formal trademark clearance and secure a
domain. The product + architecture are name-agnostic, so a rebrand is low-cost.
